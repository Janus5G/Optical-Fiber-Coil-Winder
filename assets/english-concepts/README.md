# English-adapted concept illustrations

These two images are English-language **new illustrative renderings**, adapted from the original owner-supplied Danish concept illustrations. They are **not byte-identical translations, engineering drawings, manufactured hardware or evidence of measurements**. The original Danish owner-provided image bytes are kept untouched in `../original-concept-images/` and registered in its `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`.

- `budget-en.png`: English-adapted Budget V1 conceptual poster. Its "900–1,760 kr" is the **original design-stage budget goal**, **not** the current line-item allowance (DKK 2,242). Consult the current BOM in `budget-v1/bom/bom.csv` for documented estimates.
- `standard-en.png`: English-adapted Standard/Premium V1 conceptual poster. The 2–10+ km fiber classes are **long-term illustration targets**, not qualified machine capacities. Under modeled assumptions, 10 km requires suitable thin-coated fiber; physical performance remains unverified.

**All original dimensions, fiber ratings, component fit, safety functions and operating claims must be validated against the actual CAD/BOM and physical tests.** Where a poster and engineering documents differ, the engineering documents govern.
