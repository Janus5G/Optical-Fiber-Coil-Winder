# DRAFT — Owner approval required: research/evaluation hardware terms

**NOT IN FORCE. NOT AN OSI-APPROVED OPEN-SOURCE LICENSE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**

The project owner wishes to permit research, noncommercial education and maker prototype evaluations while reserving commercial manufacturing and patent rights. The following is a discussion draft only. It must be reviewed and expressly adopted by the copyright owner and preferably by qualified legal counsel before public release.

**Draft terms for discussion:** Subject to the conditions below, the copyright owner would grant a nonexclusive, worldwide, no-charge license to copy project documentation, study designs, build and test noncommercial evaluation prototypes, modify materials for research use and publish research findings with attribution, provided applicable third-party rights are respected and no commercial sales, production-for-hire, fee-based hardware services or commercial product incorporation occur without a separate written commercial agreement. Public redistribution of files or modified designs would require preservation of copyright, notices, origin and modification disclosures; no sublicensing or trademark rights would be conveyed. Safety regulations and all applicable laws would remain the responsibility of implementers. The materials would be provided without warranty or certification. Express patent permissions, if any, would require separate carefully drafted language; **no implied patent license** is intended. Separate written permission would be required for commercial use.

**Unresolved legal decisions:** what counts as academic, institutional sponsored research, cost-recovery maker kits, internal commercial R&D; whether derivatives may be shared; patent ownership/claims and patent grant scope; liability limitations and jurisdiction; trademark use; export and safety compliance; rights to original images and third-party CAD libraries; termination and cure. These MUST be decided before adoption.

Do not merge this draft into `LICENSE` until a signed/recorded owner approval exists. Do not use OSI/open-source badges for a noncommercial restriction.
